# Integration note, 2026-09-20

## RAND formal model of agency erosion (Moon and Boudreaux, RRA4817-1, 2026)
"A Formal Model of How Artificial Intelligence Erodes Human Agency" gives afairi a formal, social-choice-theoretic backing for the agenda-control mechanism it already treats as first-class.

What to adopt:
- Three mechanisms of collective agency erosion: disenfranchisement (humans automated out of decisions), AI enfranchisement (AIs become de facto members of the deciding group), and agenda control (AI shapes which options humans see without voting). afairi's agenda-setting attack is the agenda-control case; cite RAND as the formal model.
- The decision-power-distribution metric and the irreversibility threshold: RAND models the point beyond which shifts in decision power become irreversible. Adopt this as the quantitative target for the enforcement/outage framing, so "erosion" has a tracked number rather than a narrative.
- The uncomfortable result to keep honest: even aligned AI that reliably produces good decisions erodes collective agency by displacing human participation. This strengthens the index's case that capability alone is not the safety variable.

Cross-link: this is the collective scale of the individual-scale bifurcation model in the bounded-influence repo (per-session dose budget is the individual primitive that prevents RAND's collective erosion).
